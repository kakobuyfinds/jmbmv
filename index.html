Three short reads

Better questions make a better shortlist.

BoonBuy finds, BoonBuy product links, BoonBuy QC photos and a BoonBuy shopping guide describe different parts of the same research process. Discovery brings an item into view; evidence helps you decide whether to keep it there.

Record the source, selected size, colour, measurements and unresolved questions together. When the listing changes, your notes should make it clear which version you compared.

Field note 01 / Fit

BoonBuy sizing guide: measure the garment, then choose the label

A familiar letter size can mean different things across listings. Lay a garment you like flat, measure it consistently and compare it with the seller's chart. Check whether a chest measurement is a flat width or a full circumference.

  • Hoodie size chart comparison: check chest width, shoulder placement, sleeve length and space for an underlayer.
  • T-shirt fabric weight guide: use a stated weight as one clue; also check composition, knit and garment dimensions.
  • Jacket shoulder measurement: confirm how the seller measures dropped shoulders or raglan sleeves.
  • Pants inseam comparison: compare rise, thigh width, inseam and leg opening alongside waist size.

For matching sets sizing, compare the top and bottom separately. A set may fit well in one half and poorly in the other.

Field note 02 / Evidence

BoonBuy QC photos: request evidence for a specific question

A useful QC photo checklist starts with the whole item, then moves to the detail that matters to your decision. Ask for a clear measurement or close-up when the existing images leave a question unanswered.

  • Sneaker sole inspection: compare both shoes, outsole condition, heel alignment and visible glue marks.
  • Clothing stitching inspection: look at hems, seams, pockets and fastening points under clear lighting.
  • Jersey print inspection: review placement, visible cracking, lettering and the chosen name or number.
  • Headwear circumference guide: confirm adjustment range and crown depth as well as the stated size.

Photographs cannot prove authenticity, hidden construction or long-term durability. For branded items, assess provenance and seek qualified authentication when needed.

Field note 03 / Planning

BoonBuy shopping guide: build a complete cost estimate

An item price is only one input. A useful international shipping estimate also needs the destination, parcel dimensions, billable weight and available route. Confirm the current purchasing service's charges and restrictions before paying.

  • Parcel volumetric weight: bulky packaging can affect the billed weight; check the carrier's calculation.
  • Consolidated shipping comparison: compare the packed result and service terms rather than assuming one box is cheaper.
  • Clothing return policy check: establish deadlines, exclusions and responsibility for return freight.
  • Product link verification: reopen the source and confirm the chosen variant, stock and current terms.

Include domestic delivery, service charges, currency conversion and applicable import charges. This guide does not quote a shipping rate or promise a delivery date.

A practical comparison sheet

What to check by category

Use the table as a compact buying checklist. Save the measurements and evidence that would help you choose between two similar listings.

Category Compare first Useful follow-up
Shoes Model, width, stated sizing method and selected colourway. Request a shoe measurement photo if the chart leaves fit uncertain.
Hoodies / Sweaters Chest, sleeve length, composition and desired layering room. Check cuff construction and the stated garment care instructions.
T-Shirts Body length, shoulder width, collar shape and material. Compare the measurement chart with a washed tee you already own.
Jackets Shoulder construction, lining, closure and sleeve length. Review zipper details and distinguish a style description from a tested weather rating.
Pants / Shorts Waist, rise, thigh width, inseam and hem opening. Check whether the listed waist is relaxed, stretched or adjustable.
Headwear Circumference, crown depth, brim shape and adjustment. Ask for the adjustment range when the listing says “one size.”
Sets Separate top and bottom measurements. Confirm whether mixed sizes are available before choosing a set.
Underwear / Underpants Waistband range, composition and lining information. Check hygiene exclusions in the return policy before ordering.
Jersey Fit cut, chest width, length and selected personalization. Compare front and back images with the exact chosen variant.
Accessories Dimensions, material disclosure, closure and intended use. For jewelry material disclosure, ask about metal composition and plating; photos are insufficient.

A complete listing should answer your main fit and specification questions. Missing information is a reason to ask for clarification before purchasing.

What is a BoonBuy spreadsheet?

A BoonBuy spreadsheet is an organized collection of product references used for browsing and comparison. It may include seller links, images, size notes and QC references. The term does not identify one universal or official file.

Where do the category links on this page go?

The ten category links go to their matching Hipobuyfind category pages. Other navigation links go to the Hipobuyfind homepage. This page does not provide BoonBuy checkout or account access.

Is this an official BoonBuy or designer-brand website?

No. This is an independent discovery and research guide. No affiliation with BoonBuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci, Hermès or Dior is claimed. Editorial images do not establish stock, authenticity or endorsement.

Can QC photos prove that an item is authentic?

No. QC photos can reveal visible details and some defects, but they cannot establish authenticity, material composition, durability or hidden construction. Check provenance and obtain qualified authentication when needed.

How should I compare sizes across different listings?

Compare the listing's measurements and measurement method with a garment or pair of shoes that fits you. Check units, selected variant and stated tolerances. A familiar size label alone is insufficient.

Does a product price include international shipping?

Do not assume it does. Confirm item cost, domestic delivery, service charges, currency conversion, international freight and applicable import charges in the current purchasing service before paying.

Can I download an Excel file from this page?

No. This page is an HTML buying guide with category navigation. It does not provide a downloadable Excel file or a synchronized product feed.
